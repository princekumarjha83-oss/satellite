Sentinel-2 Multispectral Ingestion

Processes European Space Agency (ESA) Sentinel-2 Level-2A imagery bands B04 (Red, 665nm) and B08 (NIR, 842nm) at native 10m spatial resolution.

Formula: NDVI = (NIR - Red) / (NIR + Red)
📈

Temporal Delta & Anomaly Detection

Computes pixel-wise bi-temporal differentials across sequential observation windows with automated cloud and water masking.

Output: Deforestation & Regrowth GeoTIFFs
